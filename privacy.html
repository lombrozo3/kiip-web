Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Kiip is an Android app and website for saving links, images and text, and listening to them. Your library belongs to you and is kept on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. Kiip has no server of its own, so nobody running Kiip can see what you save.

What Kiip stores, and where

  • On your phone: everything you save, along with its tags and notes, and the text and images Kiip keeps from links.
  • In your Google Drive: if you connect it, a folder called Kiip Library holds a copy of your library for each device, a dated backup for each of the last 30 days, the saved images, and the kept text of articles.
  • In your browser: the website keeps your library in the open page only. It also remembers your reading speed and voice, and holds your Google sign-in until the browser session ends.

How Kiip uses your Google account

Kiip asks for Google's drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created itself. It can't see the rest of your Drive. It uses this only to sync and back up your library, and to show that library to you on the website.

Your Google data isn't sold, used for advertising, or passed to anyone else. Kiip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What leaves your device

Kiip adds no analytics or advertising of its own. Information goes to other services only as follows:

  • Websites you save: Kiip's phone app downloads saved links to fetch their titles, images and text, and the website shows their preview images, so those sites see an ordinary visit.
  • Internet Archive: when a page is blocked, gone or has no readable text, the phone app sends its address to the Internet Archive to look for a saved copy.
  • Anthropic: only if you add your own Claude API key and choose Claude search. Each such search sends your saved items' titles, summaries, links, tags and the start of their text to Anthropic, under your own account. The key stays on your phone and is never synced.
  • Browser voices: the website reads aloud with your browser's voices. Some browsers' online voices send the text to the browser's maker, so choose a voice installed on your device if you'd prefer to avoid that.

Search on the phone and article summaries run on the phone itself.

Removing your data

  • Delete items for good in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything on the phone.
  • Delete the Kiip Library folder from your Google Drive to remove everything there.
  • Remove Kiip's access at any time from your Google Account.

Changes and questions

Any change to this policy will appear on this page, with a new date at the top. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
